How to read it

The teal arrow is the table's weight. It holds the table down with a lever arm equal to half the footprint width.

The violet arrow is the person pressing on the edge. Its lever arm runs from the edge of the base to where they press.

The safety factor is the holding moment divided by the tipping moment. Above 1 the table stays put. A factor of 1.5 to 2 leaves room for a bumped load, a soft rug and an uneven floor.

What this uses

  • Walnut top, 116 × 46 × 1.5 in, about 175 lb.
  • Pedestal base 73 in long, 28.5 in tall, weight set by the slider.
  • Ultralight MDF base at 32 lb/ft³. Volume from SketchUp: 6.99 ft³ at 14 in wide, 8.87 ft³ at 16 in and 9.19 ft³ at 18 in, with straight lines between and beyond those widths. The optional steel plate is 1/2 in thick, 48 in long and 2 in narrower than the base, flat on the floor inside it. Its weight is steel (0.284 lb/in³) minus the MDF it replaces (32 lb/ft³).
  • Load pressed at mid-length on the long edge, the weakest spot.
  • Rigid, level floor. A soft rug lowers the real margin.
